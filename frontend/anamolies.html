Why these routes are anomalies

Isolation Forest

Isolation Forest isolates anomalies by randomly splitting features. Points that are isolated quickly (fewer splits needed) are anomalies — they are statistically different from the bulk of routes. Common reasons a route gets flagged here:

Premium type in wrong zone
A Volvo or Vajra route in a peripheral zone is unusual — premium routes almost always run through Central.
Very high hub + unique destination
Routes from KR Market or Kempegowda to destinations served by only 1 route are structurally isolated.
Circular Volvo / premium
A circular route (origin = destination) that is also a premium service type is extremely rare and gets flagged.
